ABOUT SURU COLLECTION

Tradition, with a modern touch.

Suru Collection is a women's fashion destination based in Gaur, Rautahat, Nepal. We bring together beautiful ethnic and traditional clothing sourced with an eye for quality, style and value.

Our range is designed to grow with our customers — from everyday elegance to festive, wedding and special-occasion looks.
